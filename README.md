# The-School-Board

What it is: 
The School Board is an interactive learning game focused on actually being fun and addictive to play. You simply paste in your own studying materials, and then the game turns those materials into a game show. With different challenges and with higher and higher stakes each round, who knows what your high score will be. 

Why: 
Learning games feel stale now, they lack flavor, creativity, the want to play it over something else, that is where The School Board comes in. The goal is to create an experience where you don't think about learning, you think about playing the game. 

The Plan: 
The player will enter their name. 
Then, the player pastes their study material into the game. 
The game then builds study questions from that material. 
A host with pre-written dialogue walks you throughout the game. 
Finally the player earns rewards and see's how they did at the end. 

Who: 
This is primarily for high school and college students, though middle schoolers that can comfortably work a computer could use this too!
